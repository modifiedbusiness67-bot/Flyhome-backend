## 🗣️ TE REO MĀORI PRONUNCIATION

Key cultural terms (pronunciation guides included in documentation):
- **Aotearoa** (Ah-oh-teh-AH-roh-ah) - Land of the Long White Cloud
- **Whānau** (FAH-nah-oo) - Family / Extended family
- **Iwi** (EE-wee) - Tribe / Collective
- **Kai moana** (KAI MOH-ah-nah) - Seafood
- **Mahi** (MAH-hee) - Work / Labor
- **Te reo Māori** (teh RAY-oh MAH-oh-ree) - The Māori language

See `narration_recording_guide.md` for complete pronunciation guide (31 terms).

### Narration Audio - Choose Your Path

**OPTION A: AI Text-to-Speech (Fastest)**
- Tools: Google Translate TTS, Eleven Labs free tier
- Time: 30 minutes
- Quality: Professional, clear
- Cost: Free
- Best for: Quick production, consistent delivery

- Follow: `narration_recording_guide.md`
- Time: 1-2 hours (including recording & editing)
- Quality: Personal, authentic
- Cost: Free (home recording)
- Equipment: Microphone, headphones, Audacity

**OPTION C: Professional Narrator**
- Recommended: Native te reo Māori speaker
- Time: 5-7 days
- Quality: Exceptional, culturally authentic
- Cost: $500-2000
- Best for: Final, professional-grade production
