# AOTEAROA BEFORE: Māori Multi-Voice Documentary Script
## A Living History in Te Reo Māori & English

**Total Runtime**: 7:00 minutes  
**Speaker Format**: 6+ distinct voices across generations  
**Language**: Te reo Māori + English (bilingual conversational style)  
**Style**: Documentary dialogue - intimate, authentic, multi-generational

---

## 👥 SPEAKER ROLES & CHARACTER DESCRIPTIONS

| Role | Voice Type | Character | Sections |
|------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Kuia** (Grandmother) | Female Elder | Wisdom keeper, genealogy expert | Opening, Act 3 (culture/spirituality) |
| **Koro** (Grandfather) | Male Elder | Elder storyteller, hunter knowledge | Act 2 (hunting/mahi), closing reflection |
| **Pāpā** (Father) | Adult Male | Community builder, current knowledge | Act 1 (settlement), Act 2 (building) |
| **Whānau Member** | Adult Female | Weaver/keeper of traditions | Act 2 (gardens/weaving), Act 3 (arts) |
| **Your Son** | Young Adult Male | Bridge generation, language learner | Act 1 (journey), Act 4 (language) |
| **Tamariki 1** | Child (Boy 8-12) | Curious child, represents future | Act 2 (daily life experiences) |
| **Tamariki 2** | Child (Girl 8-12) | Young learner, cultural transmission | Act 3 (learning from elders) |

---

## 🎬 SCRIPT WITH SPEAKER ASSIGNMENTS

### OPENING SCENE - ARRIVAL & WHAKAPAPA
**[0:00 - 1:30] — Warm, reverent opening**

---

**KUIA (Grandmother):**  
"Kia koe. Ka tīmata tēnei pakipakirongo i te wā ā tō tīpuna, i te wā ā tō kuia.  
*[Hello. This story begins in the time of your ancestors.]*

E 600 taonga te wā i mua i te taenga mai o ngā kaumatua Pākehā ki Aotearoa.  
*[Over 600 years before Europeans arrived in Aotearoa.]*

I tēnei wā, he aha i hāngai ai?  
*[In those days, what was our life?]*"

**[PAUSE - visual: waaka arriving]**

---

**PĀPĀ (Father):**  
"He toiora tēnei whenua. He raumati, he āhuatanga o te moana.  
*[This was a thriving land. Beautiful seasons, the ocean all around.]*

Ā, ko ā tātau tīpuna, i haere mai i a Hawaiki ki a Aotearoa, i a Aotearoa, i a Aotearoa...  
*[Our ancestors, they came from Hawaiki to Aotearoa...]*

I te waaka, i te waaka tūturu.  
*[In the great waaka - double-hulled canoes.]*"

**[PAUSE - visual montage of voyage]**

---

**YOUR SON:**  
"Ko te ngā whakatupuranga, ko te whānau, ko te iwi...  
*[The generations, the family, the tribe...]*

I kite i a Aotearoa ko te whenua e kore e mutu. Ko te ngahere, ko te moana, ko te kai e karekare noa.  
*[They saw Aotearoa as endless land. The forests, the ocean, food everywhere.]*"

**[PAUSE]**

---

**KUIA:**  
"Ko tōku whakapapa, ko ōu whakapapa, ko tō mātau whakapapa whānui...  
*[My genealogy, your genealogy, our collective genealogy...]*

I tīmata i a Te Moana-nui-a-Kiwa. I huri i te whenua. I noho. I tupu.  
*[Started across the great ocean. We explored this land. We settled. We grew.]*

By 1600, ko Aotearoa tēnei - tō tātau whenua. Tō tātau mahi. Tō tātau whakatupuranga.  
*[By 1600, Aotearoa was ours. Our land. Our work. Our generations.]*"

---

### ACT 1: SETTLEMENT & IWIWHANAU
**[1:30 - 2:15] — Grounded, community-focused**

---

**PĀPĀ:**  
"I noho te iwi i roto i te whenua. Ko Ngāpuhi ki te raki, ko Ngāti Tūwharetoa ki waenganui, ko Ngāi Tahu ki te tonga.  
*[The iwi settled across the land. Ngāpuhi in the north, Ngāti Tūwharetoa in the middle, Ngāi Tahu in the south.]*

Ia iwi, ia iwi, he tuakiri, he toiora, he mea noa.  
*[Each iwi, each one had identity, wellness, peace.]*"

**[VISUAL: Iwi territories shown]**

---

**WHĀNAU MEMBER (Woman):**  
"Ko te whānau - ehara i te whakatupuranga kotahi anake.  
*[The whānau - not just one generation.]*

Ko te whānau, he here, he mea hōhonu. Ko te whenua, ko te moana, ko te ngahere - kō ngā mea e tautoko ai te whānau.  
*[The whānau, it's a bond, something deep. The land, the ocean, the forests - these are what support the whānau.]*

Ko tātau, ko tangata whenua. Ko tātau. Nōu mai.  
*[We are tangata whenua. We are. Forever.]*"

**[PAUSE for reflection]**

---

**KORO (Grandfather):**  
"Ko te reo Māori, te reo o ā tātau tīpuna.  
*[The Māori language, the language of our ancestors.]*

Me kōrero Māori anake. Kāo atu he reo kē.  
*[We spoke only Māori. No other language.]*

Te reo Māori - ko tēnei, he oranga, he mauri.  
*[The Māori language - this is life, this is essence.]*"

---

### ACT 2: KAI & MAHI - SUBSISTENCE & WORK
**[2:15 - 4:30] — Active, rhythmic, practical knowledge**

---

**TAMARIKI 1 (Young Boy):**  
"Koro! Koro! Me pēhea te kai?  
*[Grandpa! Grandpa! How do we get food?]*

I te raumati, i te whea? I te takurua, i te whea?  
*[In summer, where? In winter, where?]*"

**[PAUSE - grandfather smiles]**

---

**KORO:**  
"Āe, tamariki, kia whakarongo. Ko te kūmara tuatahi.  
*[Yes, child, listen. The kūmara first.]*

I te pōkaitahi, e piki ana te raumati. Ko te whenua, kia mahi, kia whakarite.  
*[At the spring equinox, the warm season rises. The soil, we work it, we prepare it.]*

Ko te kūmara - ko tēnei kai tūpuna i kawea mai i Hawaiki.  
*[The kūmara - this ancestral food brought from Hawaiki.]*

Ka tipu, ka tipu, ka roa te raumati. Ā, i te whare - e kai, e kai ai tātau.  
*[It grows, grows, through the warm season. And in the house - we eat, we nourish ourselves.]*"

**[VISUAL: Kūmara gardens]**

---

**TAMARIKI 2 (Young Girl):**  
"Ko te whānau wahine - me tahi tātau?  
*[The women of the whānau - what do we do?]*"

---

**WHĀNAU MEMBER (Woman):**  
"Āe, e tama wahine. Ko tātau, ko ngā rangatira o te mahi.  
*[Yes, young girl. We are leaders of the work.]*

Ko te raranga - ko tēnei mea nui. Ko te harikoa, ko te piupiu, ko ngā kete, ko ngā mea katoa.  
*[The weaving - this is important. The baskets, the mats, the clothes, all things.]*

Ko te lima, ko te whakaaro, ko te mōhio o ngā tīpuna - koi roto i tēnei mahi.  
*[The hands, the mind, the knowledge of ancestors - all in this work.]*

Kia tūhia e āu, kia toiora, kia pai. Ko tēnei, he tuakiri.  
*[Weave it with care, with wellness, with beauty. This is identity.]*"

**[VISUAL: Women weaving with flax]**

---

**PĀPĀ:**  
"Ko te kai moana - ehara i te mea iti.  
*[The seafood - not a small thing.]*

Ko te tāngata, ka titiro ki te moana. Ko te kina, ko te pāua, ko te pipi, ko te tuatua.  
*[The people, we look to the ocean. The sea urchin, the abalone, the mussels, the shellfish.]*

Ka neke haere, ka hōia te kupenga, ka hara i te moana.  
*[We wade out, we cast the net, we gather from the ocean.]*

Ko te kai moana - ko tēnei, he toiora, he tapu.  
*[The seafood - this is wellness, this is sacred.]*"

**[VISUAL: Coastal gathering scenes]**

---

**KORO:**  
"Ā, ko te moa. Ko te manu nui o Aotearoa.  
*[And the moa. The great bird of Aotearoa.]*

I te whare, ka whānau, ka kai. Ko te ngau, ko te kōpū, ko te huruhuru - koi te whare katoa.  
*[In the house, we survive, we eat. The meat, the organs, the feathers - throughout the whole house.]*

Ko te whakaaro o te tīpuna - kia kore e takohia te mea. Kia oti te tapu.  
*[The ancestors' thinking - nothing should be wasted. The sacred should be complete.]*

Ko tēnei, ko te manaakitanga - ki te manu, ki te whenua, ki a tātau.  
*[This is manaakitanga - care for the bird, the land, for ourselves.]*"

**[PAUSE - hunting scene visual]**

---

**TAMARIKI 1:**  
"Koro! Ko te whare - pēhea te hanga?  
*[Grandpa! The house - how is it built?]*"

---

**PĀPĀ:**  
"Ko te whare, ko tēnei taonga tūturu.  
*[The house, this is an enduring treasure.]*

Ko te rākau o te whenua, ko te kuru, ko te harakeke - koi roto i te whare.  
*[The wood of the land, the bark, the flax - all part of the house.]*

Ko te whakatupu, ko te here, ko te mahi toiora. Ko tēnei, he whare e ora ai te whānau.  
*[The building, the lashing, the careful work. This is a house where the whānau thrives.]*"

**[VISUAL: Whare construction]**

---

### ACT 3: WAIRUA, CULTURE & SPIRITUAL KNOWLEDGE
**[4:30 - 5:45] — Reverent, mystical, authoritative**

---

**KUIA:**  
"Ko te whakapapa - ehara i te kupu anake.  
*[Genealogy - it's not just words.]*

Ko te whakapapa, ko te hononga ki te atua, ki te tīpuna, ki te whenua.  
*[Genealogy connects us to the gods, to ancestors, to the land.]*

Me kōrero, me whakaputa, me tipu ki te mokopuna. Ko tēnei, he tapu, he mea nui.  
*[We speak it, we express it, we pass it to the grandchildren. This is sacred, this is important.]*

Ko tōku whakapapa, ko ōu whakapapa - ko tēnei, ehara i te mate. Ko tēnei, ko te toiora nui.  
*[My genealogy, your genealogy - this isn't death. This is great wellness.]*"

**[VISUAL: Whakapapa scroll/visualization]**

---

**PĀPĀ:**  
"Ko te tohunga - ko te tangata mōhio. Ko te tohunga whakairo, ko te tohunga kōrero, ko te tohunga tapua.  
*[The tohunga - the learned person. The master carver, the keeper of stories, the spiritual expert.]*

I a ia, ko te mōhio o ngā raumati, o ngā whakatupuranga. Ko te mana o te atua, ko te wairua o te whenua.  
*[In them, knowledge of seasons and generations. The mana of the gods, the spirit of the land.]*

Ko te kōrero - me whakarongo, me akoranga. Ko tēnei, he hui, he whakaakoranga tuakiri.  
*[The talk - we listen, we learn. This is gathering, this is identity education.]*"

---

**WHĀNAU MEMBER (Woman):**  
"Ko te whakairo - ko tēnei, he mahi rangatira.  
*[Carving - this is chiefly work.]*

Ko te piripiri, ko te kupika, ko te tohu o te tīpuna - koi roto i te pou, i te whakairo.  
*[The patterns, the designs, the marks of ancestors - all in the post, in the carving.]*

Ko tēnei, ehara i te āhuatanga anake. Ko tēnei, ko te hītori e whakapakoko ana.  
*[This isn't just decoration. This is history carved into wood.]*"

**[VISUAL: Carving workshop]**

---

**KORO:**  
"Ko te karakia - ko tēnei, ko te pōkaitahi o te mea katoa.  
*[The prayer - this is the foundation of everything.]*

I mua i te mahi, i te raumati, i te kai, i te mate - karakia. Ko te karakia, ko tēnei e hono ai tātau ki te atua, ki te whenua.  
*[Before work, before hunting, before eating, before death - karakia. Prayer connects us to the gods, to the land.]*

Ko Tangaroa, ko te Moana. Ko Tāne, ko te Ngahere. Ko Papatūānuku, ko te Whenua Mātua.  
*[Tangaroa, the Ocean. Tāne, the Forest. Papatūānuku, the Earth Mother.]*

Ko ngā atua - koi ngā mea katoa. Ko te mauri o te manu, ko te mauri o te ahi - tapu.  
*[The gods - in all things. The essence of the bird, the essence of fire - sacred.]*"

**[VISUAL: Spiritual ceremonies, karakia moments]**

---

**TAMARIKI 2:**  
"Ko te haka - he aha tēnei?  
*[What is the haka?]*"

---

**PĀPĀ:**  
"Ko te haka - ko tēnei, ko te wairua o te iwi.  
*[The haka - this is the spirit of the tribe.]*

Ko te tinana, ko te whakaaro, ko te mana - koi roto i te haka. Ko te haka, ehara i te pāpāri anake.  
*[The body, the mind, the mana - all in the haka. The haka isn't just dance.]*

Ko te haka, ko tēnei e whakapakoko ai tātau i a tātau, i ā tātau tīpuna, i te whenua.  
*[The haka expresses who we are, our ancestors, our land.]*"

**[VISUAL: Haka performance]**

---

**WHĀNAU MEMBER (Woman):**  
"Ko te waiata - ko ngā kupu o te pū.  
*[The song - words from the source/origin.]*

I te waiata, ko te aroha, ko te mate, ko te whakahari. Ko te waiata, e tipu ana te mōhio o tātau whānau.  
*[In song, love, grief, celebration. Through song, our whānau knowledge grows.]*

Ko tēnei, ko te reo Māori e whakapui ana. Ko tēnei, ko te toiora o tātau katoa.  
*[This is the Māori language expressing itself. This is the wellness of us all.]*"

---

### ACT 4: TE REO MĀORI - THE LANGUAGE OF IDENTITY
**[5:45 - 7:00] — Passionate, reflective, powerful closing**

---

**YOUR SON:**  
"Ko te reo Māori - ko tēnei, ehara i te reo anake.  
*[The Māori language - this is not just language.]*

Ko te reo Māori, ko tēnei, ko te ara, ko te wāhi, ko te āhuatanga o a tātau tīpuna.  
*[The Māori language is the way, the place, the essence of our ancestors.]*

Ko te moana - ehara i te 'ocean' anake. Ko te moana, ko te hononga ki te toiora, ki te kai, ki te wairua.  
*[The ocean - it's not just 'ocean'. The moana is connection to wellness, food, spirit.]*

Ko te tangata whenua - ko tēnei, ehara i te kupu mokemoke. Ko tēnei, ko te tuakiri tūturu o a tātau.  
*[Tangata whenua - this isn't just words. This is our enduring identity.]*"

**[PAUSE - reflective visual]**

---

**KUIA:**  
"Ko te reo - ko tēnei, ko te ahi o ā tātau tīpuna.  
*[The language - this is the fire of our ancestors.]*

I mua i te 1600, ko te reo Māori anake. Ko te reo Māori i te pōkaitahi, i te waenganui, i te mutunga - koi te mea katoa.  
*[Before 1600, only Māori language. Māori language at the beginning, in the middle, at the end - in all things.]*

Ko te whānau tamariki - ko te reo Māori, te reo tuatahi. Ko te reo o te pūtātā, ko te reo o te hui, ko te reo o te karakia.  
*[The children of the whānau - Māori was their first language. The language of the fire, of gatherings, of prayers.]*"

---

**KORO:**  
"Ko te manaakitanga - he pono, he whakarite ki te tangata katoa.  
*[Manaakitanga - is truth, care for all people.]*

Ko te kaitiakitanga - ko tēnei, ko te kawe o te whenua. Kāo atu he taonga roa, ko tēnei ka tipu, ka atua ai.  
*[Kaitiakitanga - this is guardianship of the land. Not ownership, but this grows, this becomes spiritual.]*

Ko te whanaungatanga - ko ngā here o te whakatupu, o te whenua, o te tangata.  
*[Whanaungatanga - the bonds of growth, land, and people.]*

Ko ngā kupu Māori katoa - koi roto te mōhio o te Polynesia, te ao o ā tātau tīpuna.  
*[All Māori words - contain the knowledge of Polynesia, the world of our ancestors.]*"

---

**ALL SPEAKERS (Layered voices - building together):**

**KUIA:** Ko Aotearoa tō tātau...  
*[Aotearoa is ours...]*

**PĀPĀ:** Ko te whenua tō tātau...  
*[The land is ours...]*

**WHĀNAU MEMBER:** Ko te wairua tō tātau...  
*[The spirit is ours...]*

**KORO:** Ko te reo Māori tō tātau...  
*[The Māori language is ours...]*

**YOUR SON:** Ko te toiora tō tātau...  
*[Wellness is ours...]*

**TAMARIKI 1 & 2:** Ko te whenua, ko te whakatupuranga, ko te tīpuna!  
*[The land, the generations, the ancestors!]*

---

**[EXTENDED PAUSE - emotional moment]**

---

**KUIA (Closing - soft, profound):**  
"Ko te reo Māori, i kore e mate. I mate te pakipakirongo, i mate te tīpuna - engari ko te reo, ko te reo Māori, i ora tonu.  
*[The Māori language never died. The stories died, the ancestors passed - but the language, the Māori language, it lived on.]*

Ā, i tēnei raumati, i tēnei whakatupuranga, kua oho anō tātau. Kua kōrero anō tātau. Kua whakarite anō tātau ki te reo o ā tātau tīpuna.  
*[And now, in this season, in this generation, we have awakened. We speak again. We have prepared ourselves for the language of our ancestors.]*

Ko tēnei, ko te toiora. Ko tēnei, ko te aroha. Ko tēnei, ko te mana o a tātau katoa.  
*[This is wellness. This is love. This is the mana of us all.]*

Kia ora.  
*[Thank you.]*"

---

**[Final visual: Stars over Aotearoa, multigenerational whānau together]**

## 📝 SCRIPT SPECIFICATIONS FOR RECORDING

### Language Distribution
- **Te reo Māori ONLY**: 55-60% of dialogue
- **English ONLY**: 10-15% of dialogue  
- **Bilingual (Māori + English)**: 25-30% of dialogue
- **Māori phrases**: All major cultural concepts spoken in te reo first

### Pronunciation Notes
**All speakers MUST pronounce te reo Māori correctly** — This is non-negotiable for cultural authenticity.

| Speaker | Tone | Pace | Energy | Emotional Quality |
|---------|------|------|--------|------------------|
| **Kuia** | Wise, measured, authoritative | Slow, deliberate | Calm, grounded | Reverent, ancestral |

| **Tamariki 2 (Girl)** | Thoughtful, engaged learner | Moderate | Active, attentive | Respectful, engaged |
